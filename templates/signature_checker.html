Signature / Document Checker

Compare the normal UKG Employee Information roster to employee PDF filenames such as A. Priest.pdf or J. Smith.pdf.

What to upload

Step 1 — UKG: Run and save your normal UKG Employee Information roster as Excel (.xlsx).

Step 2 — PDF names: Open the folder containing the signed PDFs in File Explorer. Click the address bar, type cmd, and press Enter.

In the black Command Prompt window, type:

dir /b *.pdf > PDF-List.txt

Press Enter. A file named PDF-List.txt will appear in that folder. Upload it below. You do not need to upload the signed PDFs.
